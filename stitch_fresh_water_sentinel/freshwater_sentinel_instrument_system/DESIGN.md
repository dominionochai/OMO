## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the clinical precision, authority, and cinematic gravity of deep-field environmental intelligence and oceanographic instrumentation. Built for hydrologists, environmental monitoring agencies, and ecological intelligence operators, the aesthetic treats digital displays as specialized optical apertures into planetary dynamics.

The emotional baseline is absolute operational clarity, sober urgency, and intellectual rigor. The design eschews decorative visual clutter, rounded novelty, and saturated distraction in favor of razor-sharp structural geometry, deep obsidian fields, high-density monospaced telemetry, and precise hairline cartography. Surfaces feel like anti-reflective coated sapphire lenses and laboratory display panels functioning in low-light research outposts.

Key tenets:
- **Zero Decorative Softness**: Absolute adherence to 0px radii across all operational surfaces, cards, viewports, and interactive targets.
- **Instrumental Contrast**: Information density calibrated via crisp hairline dividers (`#1a232c`, `#263342`), deliberate negative space, and disciplined luminescence against an obsidian abyss.
- **Spectral Functionalism**: Sparing application of photon-derived accents (cobalt, spectral cyan, emerald, amber, crimson) reserved exclusively for verified state, delta magnitude, and sensor alerts.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for prolonged operational use in dark field environments, maximizing signal-to-noise ratio and optical fidelity:

### Deep Ground & Structural Containers
- **Deep Obsidian (Base Surface)**: `#070b0e` — The baseline viewport ground; non-reflective optical darkness.
- **Obsidian Tier 1 (Surface Elevated)**: `#0c1217` — Primary operational tiles, telemetry pods, and data tables.
- **Obsidian Tier 2 (Hover/Active Canvas)**: `#121b22` — Active row states, transient overlays, and selected inspect nodes.
- **Structural Hairlines**:
  - Subtle Grid / Perimeter: `#1a232c` (1px solid default)
  - Structural Division / Focus Delimiters: `#263342` (1px solid active/hover)

### Telemetry & Text Tokens
- **Signal Alpha (Text Primary)**: `#f8fafc` — Primary readings, active coordinate values, critical headlines.
- **Signal Beta (Text Secondary)**: `#94a3b8` — Metric labels, unit denotations, persistent navigation.
- **Signal Gamma (Text Tertiary / Grid Data)**: `#475569` — Ticks, calibration rules, non-critical indices, disabled states.

### Instrumental Accents & Telemetric States
- **Hydrological Focus (Primary)**: `#0ea5e9` — Baseline telemetry, focus indicators, active operational channels.
- **Spectral Cyan (Secondary)**: `#38bdf8` — Waveform peaks, live vector streams, active geographic overlays.
- **Biosphere Emerald (Tertiary/Nominal)**: `#10b981` — Stable equilibrium, optimal sensor health, uncontaminated thresholds.
- **Desiccation Amber (Advisory Alert)**: `#f59e0b` — Cautionary deviation, seasonal anomaly, sensor recalibration window.
- **Critical Crimson (Emergency Alert)**: `#ef4444` — Severe contamination, critical flow collapse, operational failure.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a deliberate dichotomy between clinical human communication and machine-generated telemetry:

- **Primary Sans (`Inter`)**: Governs editorial synthesis, executive readouts, navigation architecture, and human commentary. Set at medium-to-semibold weights with tightened tracking at display scales to maintain cinematic gravitas.
- **Technical Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Mandated for all numerical coordinates, sensor IDs, timecode stamps, spectral band readings, delta quantifications, and status indicators. Monospaced characters guarantee that live sensor tickers update without horizontal layout jitter.
- **Case Conventions**: Section indices, geographic markers, and telemetry tags operate in rigid uppercase (`text-transform: uppercase`) with amplified tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mimic physical laboratory equipment silk-screening.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to a rigid Cartesian instrumentation grid:

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column structural grid for desktop (min 1280px), 8-column for intermediate data consoles (768px–1279px), and 4-column stack for handheld telemetry devices (<768px).
- **Edge Cadence**: Viewports maximize actionable operational area. Outer margins stay tight (`margin-desktop: 2.5rem`, `margin-mobile: 1rem`), framed by perimeter hairlines.
- **Rhythm & Gaps**: Structural modules lock into `1rem` and `1.5rem` gutter increments. Interior component padding maintains strict 4px/8px incremental steps (`space-xs` through `space-xl`).
- **Data Densification**: Metric tables and real-time feeds collapse spacing to `space-xs` and `space-sm` with hairline border containment, enabling high information density across multi-display setups without scroll degradation.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system completely rejects organic drop shadows, multi-layered diffuse blurs, and skeuomorphic embossing. Operational depth is communicated purely through **optical plane sequencing** and **luminescence gradients**:

- **Depth by Hairline Enclosure**: Visual stratification occurs entirely through 1px boundaries (`#1a232c` for inactive/background containers; `#263342` for active or focused modules).
- **Surface Luminance Stepping**:
  - Level 0 (Base Abyssal Viewport): `#070b0e`
  - Level 1 (Primary Instrument Canvas): `#0c1217`
  - Level 2 (Sub-panel / Flyout / Active Row): `#121b22`
- **Spectral Edge Accents**: Selected inspect states utilize a 1px solid active line (`#0ea5e9`) on the leading or top edge rather than volumetric elevation shadows.
- **Phosphor Bloom (Restrained)**: In critical alert conditions, elements may project an ultra-tight, sharp photonic outline (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #ef4444`) without atmospheric blur spread.

## Shapes

The design system enforces **Level 0 (Sharp)** roundedness across every visual primitive without exception.

- **Zero Radii**: All border radii evaluate to `0px` (`rounded-none`). This applies to buttons, inputs, modal containers, alert banners, data chips, and interactive cards.
- **Technical Precision**: The razor-sharp edge profile reflects military, scientific, and industrial console standards. Corner terminals meet at absolute 90-degree orthogonal intersections.
- **Notched Variants (Optional Specialized Variant)**: For high-priority optical framing or sensor HUD corners, an optional 45-degree corner chamfer (3px × 3px) may be deployed via CSS clip-path to signify calibrated optical framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary / Telemetric Trigger**: Background `#0ea5e9`, text `#070b0e`, font JetBrains Mono 12px semibold uppercase. Zero border radius. Hover state transitions to `#38bdf8` with zero easing duration (instant laboratory feedback).
- **Secondary / Instrument Command**: Background `#0c1217`, border 1px solid `#263342`, text `#f8fafc`. Hover: border `#0ea5e9`, text `#38bdf8`.
- **Danger / Abort Trigger**: Background transparent, border 1px solid `#ef4444`, text `#ef4444`. Hover: background `#ef4444`, text `#070b0e`.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- Structural rectangular tags (`roundedness: 0`).
- Background: `#070b0e`, border: 1px solid `#1a232c`, font: JetBrains Mono 10px uppercase tracking-wide.
- Includes a 4px × 4px square status beacon on the leading edge (Emerald `#10b981` for active nominal, Amber `#f59e0b` for advisory, Crimson `#ef4444` for excursion).

### Lists & Telemetry Feeds
- Hairline-delimited rows separated by 1px solid `#1a232c`.
- Fixed height per line (`36px` compact, `44px` expanded). Alternating zebra striping is prohibited; hover utilizes `#121b22`.
- Numeric values right-aligned using tabular figures in JetBrains Mono.

### Inputs & Sensor Modifiers
- Background `#070b0e`, border 1px solid `#1a232c`, text `#f8fafc`, font Inter 14px.
- Focus state: border 1px solid `#0ea5e9` with zero shadow.
- Placeholder text in `#475569`. In-line unit descriptors (e.g., `mg/L`, `pH`, `m³/s`) displayed in JetBrains Mono 11px uppercase docked to trailing edge.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Checkbox: 14px × 14px sharp square, border 1px solid `#263342`, background `#070b0e`. Checked state fills `#0ea5e9` with a centered 6px × 6px obsidian square core instead of a decorative checkmark.
- Segment Controls: Hairline frame `#1a232c` containing zero-radius tiles. Active selection features `#121b22` background with a top 2px indicator bar in `#0ea5e9`.

### Instrument Cards & Sensor Viewports
- Flat `#0c1217` surfaces enclosed by 1px solid `#1a232c`.
- Headers feature a dedicated sub-bar: 28px height, JetBrains Mono 11px uppercase label, and trailing sensor coordinates in `#475569`.
- No floating cards or shadow lifts permitted. Cards anchor seamlessly to the master viewport grid.

### Specialized Environmental Intelligence Primitives
- **Crosshair Reticle**: 1px dashed or solid hairline targeting guides (`#263342`) overlaid on map viewports.
- **Spectral Band Indicator**: Horizontal bar strip showing parameter thresholds (e.g., salinity, dissolved oxygen) divided into hairline-separated blocks across nominal, alert, and critical zones.
