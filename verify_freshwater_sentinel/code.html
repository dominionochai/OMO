Epistemic Transparency Commitment

Freshwater Sentinel enforces epistemic transparency. When field validation data is missing, ambiguous, or clouded by atmospheric interference, events are flagged as "Unverifiable" rather than assumed correct. Predictive algorithms are never retroactively curve-fitted without recorded ground-truth soil or spectrophotometric water samples.
